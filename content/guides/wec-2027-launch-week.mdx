# WEC 2027 Launch Week: which round to book, and what it costs

*This page has two affiliate links: Airalo (eSIMs) and Omio (trains and buses). If you buy through them, PitLane Travel may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend. We don't sell tickets, and there are no ticket-seller affiliate links on this page.*

**Last checked: Sunday 8 November 2026.** Each price is marked **Confirmed** (official seller or FIA WEC) or **Reported** (press, named).

FIA WEC Launch Week runs from **Tuesday 10 to Monday 16 November 2026**, with offers on 2027 tickets. Each circuit sets its own offer, and several rounds have been on sale for months, so this is a discount window, not the start of sales everywhere. For most UK fans the best value is the 6 Hours of Silverstone (23–25 April, weekend from £95 when sales opened in June), while Le Mans (9–13 June) went on general sale on 6 November.

## WEC Launch Week 2027 at a glance

- **When:** Tue 10 to Mon 16 Nov 2026. **Confirmed** on [fiawec.com](https://www.fiawec.com/en/page/launch-week) ("November 10–16") and by [Fuji Speedway](https://fujispeedway.jp/motorsports/article/2027_wec_ticket) ("11/16 (Mon)"). Some write-ups say it ends on "Sunday 16 November". 16 November is a Monday.
- **What's on offer:** each circuit sets its own Launch Week offer. FIA WEC's page lists the rounds but, when we read it on 8 Nov, showed no discount figures.
- **Only one price is published so far:** Fuji's **¥9,000** three-day general admission (25% off). **Confirmed.**
- **Start and end times aren't published.** Check each seller on the morning of 10 Nov.
- **Already on sale before Launch Week:** Imola, Silverstone and Spa since June, and Le Mans since 6 Nov. For those, Launch Week can only add a discount on top of prices you can already buy.

**Before 10 November**
1. Pick your round, or two, using the table below.
2. Make an account with the official seller: TicketOne for Imola (via autodromoimola.it), Silverstone's own site, ticket-aco.24h-lemans.com for Le Mans, Fuji Ticket for Fuji.
3. Press "Get notified" on fiawec.com for the rounds that aren't priced yet.
4. Note the current price, so you can tell whether the Launch Week price is really lower.
5. Read the ticket rules: app-only tickets, transfer limits, no refunds.
6. Pencil in travel and a room before you buy anything non-refundable.

## Which WEC round should you book?

### First WEC race: Silverstone
It's the obvious start for UK fans. There's no flight, the weekend was from £95 when sales opened in June, and it's the first WEC round at Silverstone since 2019; it hosted eight times between 2012 and 2019. FIA WEC says paddock and pit walk options are also available. More on where to watch: [Silverstone grandstands](/circuits/silverstone-circuit/grandstands).

### Best atmosphere: Le Mans
Le Mans is the bucket-list one. The race is 9–13 June, and the Week pass is €126, or €75 if you're an ACO member. ACO members had a presale on 3–5 Nov, and general sale opened on Fri 6 Nov, both online and at the circuit. It isn't part of the Launch Week discount story, so if you're going, book now and sort your camping or room early. [Le Mans race guide](/races/24-hours-of-le-mans-2026-wec-2026).

### Cheapest European weekend: Imola
At €55 for the weekend (FIA WEC's June from-price), Imola is the cheapest European round we can confirm. Tickets are bearer tickets, and the permanent grandstands are free seating (autodromoimola.it). [Imola race guide](/races/6-hours-of-imola-2026-wec-2026).

### Classic circuit: Spa
Spa is one of only two tracks, with Le Mans, that have been on every WEC calendar. The weekend was from €65 and Thursday plus Friday €40 when sales opened in June. It's three weeks after Silverstone. [Spa race guide](/races/totalenergies-6-hours-of-spa-francorchamps-2026-wec-2026).

### Long-haul trips: Fuji, COTA, São Paulo, Qatar, Bahrain
Only Fuji has published 2027 prices. Its Launch Week ¥9,000 three-day pass is a real 25% off the ¥12,000 general price, and the 20% Super Early Bird runs on to 30 April, so there's no need to panic-buy. For COTA, São Paulo, Qatar and Bahrain, wait for prices on 10 Nov and book flights before tickets. ## Two races, one trip

Imola and Silverstone are a fortnight apart (9–11 and 23–25 April). Silverstone and Spa are three weeks apart (23–25 April and 13–15 May). Either pair works as a spring double from the UK. For every 2027 pairing across series, see [Two Races, One Trip](/guides/two-races-one-trip-2027).

## Is the Launch Week price worth it?

It depends on the round:
- **Fuji: yes, if you're sure.** 25% off is the deepest Fuji discount, but 20% stays available until April.
- **Imola, Silverstone, Spa:** tickets are already on sale; FIA WEC announced from-prices in June. Only buy in Launch Week if the price on screen is lower than those (€55, £95 and €65 for the weekend in June).
- **Le Mans:** there's no Launch Week angle. It's simply on sale.

Know the rules before you pay. At Silverstone, tickets are **mobile-only through an app**. They can't be resold except on an official Silverstone platform (if there is one), a single ticket can be transferred **at most four times**, and there are **no refunds unless the event is cancelled**. **Confirmed** in the [Silverstone WEC 2027 terms](https://www.silverstone.co.uk/terms-and-conditions/fia-wec-terms-and-conditions-2027).

## FAQs

**When is WEC Launch Week 2026?**
From Tuesday 10 November to Monday 16 November 2026, according to FIA WEC and Fuji Speedway. Offers are set by each circuit and may end early if allocations sell out.

**How much can you save in WEC Launch Week?**
It depends on the circuit, because each one sets its own offer. Fuji, for example, offers 25% off its three-day general admission pass at ¥9,000.

**Is Silverstone back on the WEC calendar in 2027?**
Yes. The 6 Hours of Silverstone returns on 23–25 April 2027, its first WEC round since 2019, with weekend admission from £95 when sales opened in June 2026.

**Are 2027 Le Mans 24 Hours tickets on sale?**
Yes. General sale opened on Friday 6 November 2026 after an ACO member presale. The Week pass costs €126, or €75 for ACO members.

**Which WEC race is best for a first-timer from the UK?**
Silverstone is the easiest: no flight, low prices and a full weekend for under £100. Le Mans is the bucket-list choice, but it costs more and needs camping or accommodation booked early.
