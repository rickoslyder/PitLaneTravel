Tickets for the 2027 Azerbaijan Grand Prix (24–26 September) are on sale from the official promoter, Baku City Circuit, with 20% off until 16 November 2026. ## Where to buy: promoter, F1 store or package

**Baku City Circuit (the promoter)** is the main seller. It shows every grandstand, the early-bird and full prices, and what each stand includes. Its official ticketing provider is iTicket.AZ, and it has its own app.

**The F1 ticket store** ([ticketing.formula1.com/azerbaijan](https://ticketing.formula1.com/azerbaijan/)) also sells Baku and says all standard grandstand tickets include a Thursday pit lane walk. Compare its price with the promoter's on the day you buy.

**F1 Experiences** is F1's official hospitality and package seller. It lists 2027 Baku packages and takes deposits, but says 2027 package pricing isn't confirmed. A deposit isn't a ticket.

Buy only from these official sellers.

### Best seats by budget
- **Over $1,000:** Absheron C gives you the start, the pits and the finish in one view. Absheron E at Turn 1 is the pick if you want overtakes.
- **$600–$800:** Azneft at Turn 16 is the one we'd buy. It's the exit onto the long run to the line, and at $650 it's well under the Absheron stands.
- **Under $600:** Khazar ($410) for raw speed: the promoter sells it on 300 km/h-plus speeds. Sahil ($590) if you want to see two parts of the track.
- **Cheapest:** Filarmoniya at $400. The promoter sells it on braking and overtakes, but **Reported** views from fan guides are weaker here, so check the circuit map first.
- **Old City atmosphere:** Icheri Sheher sits by the castle walls. Fan guides **report** that getting in and out is slower.

Our full stand-by-stand guide is here: [Baku City Circuit grandstands](/circuits/baku-city-circuit/grandstands).

### General admission: what we know
On 8 November 2026 the promoter's page listed grandstands only, with no general admission. Don't buy a GA "ticket" from anyone else until the promoter or the F1 store lists it. Fan guides **report** that GA doesn't include the pit lane walk or concerts. That's unconfirmed, so check the listing when it appears.

## What your ticket includes

- **Pit lane walk:** every grandstand on the promoter's page lists it, and the F1 store says all standard grandstand tickets include a Thursday pit lane walk. **Confirmed.**
- **Concerts:** **not every stand lists concert access.** On 8 Nov we saw it listed on Absheron E and City. Check your own stand before you buy.
- **Wheelchair access:** listed for Absheron E and Azneft A.

## Planning the trip

### Getting there from the UK
From the UK you'll fly; compare routes into Heydar Aliyev International (GYD) and book once you have tickets. [Our view]

### Airport to the city
Heydar Aliyev International Airport (GYD) is about 30 km from the city centre by road. **Confirmed** (F1 ticket store). Plan a taxi or transfer, and allow extra time on race days for road closures around the street circuit. [Our view]

### Where to stay
The circuit runs along the Bulvar, the seafront promenade, which is within walking distance of most central hotels. **Confirmed** (F1 ticket store). Two areas to look at:
- **Bulvar and the city centre:** walk to most stands.
- **The Old City (Icheri Sheher):** atmosphere, close to the castle section, but expect closures and crowds.

Book early; central rooms go first.

### Entry requirements
Check the rules for your passport on official sources before you book: [GOV.UK Azerbaijan travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/azerbaijan) for UK travellers. We don't summarise visa rules here, because they change.
