1. **Pick your race and a back-up.** Check whether it's a sprint weekend; 10 of the 24 rounds are.
2. **Make your account before sale day:**

   - MobilityStation for Suzuka
   - an Automobile Club de Monaco ticketing account for Monaco

3. **Know the limits:**
   - Melbourne: 10 at general sale and 6 in the Amex presale (confirmed)
   - Madrid's Amex presale: 10 per cardholder (confirmed)
   - Singapore: 4 per person in the first phase (reported by trackers; check on the day)
   - Monaco: check the order limit when you log in
4. **Set your alarm in UK time.** Singapore and Suzuka both open at 02:00.
5. **Follow the queue rules.** Ticketmaster's advice for Melbourne works for most sales:
   - use one account, one device and one browser
   - turn off your VPN and any browser extensions
   - don't refresh
   - once you're in, you have 10 minutes to finish
6. **Use a presale route if you have one:**
   - Amex: Silverstone, Monza, Madrid and Melbourne
   - club membership: Silverstone's SRC, the ACM in Monaco and the Albert Park Circuit Club
   - pre-registration: Türkiye
7. **Buy only from the official seller or its listed agents.** Read Buying F1 tickets safely first.

## Who sells what: promoter, F1 store or package?

**The promoter or circuit is the main seller.** It sets the on-sale date, the presales and the prices, and it's usually the first to say anything. If a date isn't on the promoter's site, treat it as unconfirmed.

**Formula 1's official ticket store** ([ticketing.formula1.com](https://ticketing.formula1.com/)) sells many races too. Its prices and availability can differ from the promoter's, so compare both on the day you buy.

**F1 Experiences and other package sellers** bundle tickets with hospitality or hotels and may take deposits. A deposit isn't a ticket. For resale and transfer rules, see Buying F1 tickets safely.

## FAQ

**When do 2027 F1 tickets go on sale?**
There's no single on-sale date for 2027 F1 tickets: each of the 24 races is sold by its own promoter on its own schedule. As of 2 November 2026, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Australia, Miami, Monaco, Azerbaijan and most European rounds are already on sale, Singapore's general sale opens on 11 November at 10:00 Singapore time (02:00 UK) and Suzuka opens on 15 November at 11:00 Japan time (02:00 UK). Seven races, including Las Vegas, Abu Dhabi and Mexico City, haven't announced a date yet, and Canada has postponed its public sale.

**Where can I buy official 2027 F1 tickets?**
From the race promoter's own website, or from Formula 1's official ticket store at ticketing.formula1.com. Formula 1 says genuine tickets come from formula1.com, race promoters and their appointed agents. Check the promoter first, because prices and availability can differ between sellers.

**What time do Singapore and Suzuka 2027 tickets go on sale in the UK?**
Singapore's general sale opens at 10:00 Singapore time on Wednesday 11 November 2026, which is 02:00 in the UK. Suzuka opens at 11:00 Japan time on Sunday 15 November 2026, which is also 02:00 in the UK. Suzuka sells through MobilityStation, so register before sale day.

**Does joining an F1 ticket waitlist guarantee me a ticket?**
No. The F1 store's waitlist tells you when tickets drop; it isn't a reserved seat, and a deposit with a package seller isn't a ticket either. A "waitlist" button on the F1 store also doesn't mean the race isn't on sale: some promoters are already selling on their own sites.

**Which 2027 F1 races haven't announced a ticket sale date?**
As of 2 November 2026: China, Türkiye, Mexico City, São Paulo, Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi. Canada ran its presales but postponed its general public sale and hasn't set a new date. We check every race weekly and update this page.

**Why was the 2027 Canadian Grand Prix ticket sale postponed?**
The organiser says it postponed the general public sale, originally set for 24 September 2026, to finalise a few important elements of the 2027 event. It hasn't given a new date and asks fans to subscribe to its newsletter. Tickets bought in the presales are confirmed.
